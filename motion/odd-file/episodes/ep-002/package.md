## 15. Fact check

| Claim | Status | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People overestimate how much others notice their appearance and behaviour | Established | Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000), *JPSP* 78(2):211–222 |
| Students wearing an embarrassing T-shirt guessed ~half the room would notice; ~23 % did | Established (study 1) | Same paper — wearers estimated ≈46 %, observers ≈23 % |
| The effect also appears for flattering shirts and for contributions to group discussion | Established (studies 2–5) | Same paper |
| “Everyone is the main character of their own story” | Interpretation, not a finding | Frames egocentric bias; not presented as data in the film |

The figure beat shows **23 %** and the sub-line says “Students expected about 50. Fewer than 23 noticed.” — deliberately rounded language, and the caption never claims a precise 50.0.
