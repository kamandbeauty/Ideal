# Motus pistol animation reference

The user supplied `FBX_Pistol_Starter_27A (1).zip` via Google Drive (SHA-256 `9d5a6ff26a0e70fa36625b9ff3fab2cbffad7d8a4200a0ce12a225d87bcc5559`) and confirmed permission to use the files and place converted versions in this repository. The archive identifies MocapOnline/MotusMan v55; no independent license text was included. Preserve the user's permission record and do not infer broader licensing terms.

`motus-pistol-reference.glb` is a test-only conversion of the source mannequin and three FBX animations: standing pistol aim idle, walking while aiming, and standing single shot. Conversion uses assimpjs `glb2`, followed by `ci/assemble_motus_reference.py` to map animation targets by unique node name and embed the supplied `MCG_diff.jpg` texture. This conversion can alter FBX fidelity and needs visual and runtime review. It is not an authored rifle animation and no weapon or hand IK is supplied by this conversion. It has not been retargeted or integrated into hero/squad gameplay. The existing hero and its reported rig and grip problems are unchanged. The project's all-resources export may include this staged reference, increasing APK size; remove from shipping exports or choose a selective export once the asset is no longer needed for testing.

The original archive notes that MotusMan v55 and v4 skeletons differ; do not assume animations from other packs match. Test skeletal mapping and proportions explicitly before retargeting. Presence of a clip or imported bones is not visual proof of natural motion, correct two-handed rifle placement, transition blending, or phone performance.
