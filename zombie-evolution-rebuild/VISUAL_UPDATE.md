# Semi-realistic visual iteration

This is an in-progress visual update, not a claim of final art quality, Android performance, or a tested APK. The launcher uses a generated background image (not a gameplay screenshot). The level adds a procedural sky, atmospheric colors, bridge details, shared box meshes, clouds and clearer gates. The Persian UI retains its existing controls and text, with darker panels for contrast. Bridge collision, combat, progression and the portrait camera remain unchanged. The earlier correction reverses screen-drag world-X mapping and updates directional touch assertions; it still requires actual device validation.

## Character assets

The user's `2630_open3dmodel.zip` OBJ and textures were converted to GLB for the hero. Its original static T-pose received a rough vertex-space shoulder adjustment; this is not a rig and may have deformation artifacts. `13899_open3dmodel.zip` OBJ and texture were converted for the regular zombie. `28069_open3dmodel.rar` and `27189_open3dmodel.rar` contained 3ds Max files only; their base editable-mesh vertices, faces and UVs were extracted using the open-source `io_scene_max` parser in [3DConvert](https://github.com/Zigg0r/3DConvert), and the supplied TGA textures embedded in GLB. Source Z-up coordinates were converted to Y-up for the hero and the Max meshes. They are used for runner and brute appearances respectively. These are experimental static conversions; the Max conversion does not include node transforms, skeletons, skin weights or animation. The whole-mesh bob is only a placeholder, not a walk or attack animation. If a model fails to load, the existing procedural placeholder is used.

The original archives remain untouched. The user states that all supplied character models are free and licensed. The license texts and specific permissions or attribution requirements were not found in the reviewed archives and have not been independently verified; retain the relevant source and license records before distribution. The hero filename identifies Leon Kennedy.

## Validation status

A texture/mesh structure check and GDScript grammar check are possible locally, but these do not verify visual composition, engine import, animations, Persian shaping, mobile speed, or compatibility. The new `visual_assets_test.gd` attempts to load and instantiate all four models in Godot; the existing 12 headless checks remain. A fresh rendered run and Android export/device test are necessary before presenting a new APK as verified. The historic `SOURCE_MANIFEST.sha256` is preserved; `CURRENT_MANIFEST.sha256` records the current changed and new project assets.

## Prototype weapon and sound pass

A low-poly rifle with receiver, barrel, stock, grip, magazine and sight replaces the one-box gun. Each real shot triggers a short muzzle flash and an original generated rifle sound; real damage hits, gate selection, reloading, hero damage and results trigger separate generated effects. These are placeholders, not recorded or final mastered audio. Effects use a bounded pool on the existing Master bus, so the existing sound setting mutes them. Raycast origin, collision masks, damage, ammunition and shooting cadence have not been changed. `ci/create_prototype_sfx.py` generates the WAV files deterministically, and `tests/effects_test.gd` checks importing, voice selection, muzzle-flash lifetime and muting. The original static-model and license limitations above remain.

Their proportions are stylized, not validated as semi-realistic. ## Bridge and combat visual iteration

A further incremental pass replaces the flat road color with a world-space procedural asphalt shader and adds shoulder markings. Automatic shots now render tracers from the weapon's muzzle, and real damage triggers short CPU impact sparks, a brief hit lean, and a short fall on defeat. The defeat signal, score update and combat damage still happen immediately; collision is disabled on defeat while the visual fall completes. The squad uses paired columns to keep the center sightline clear. These are actual runtime effects, not pre-rendered footage. The texture is procedural rather than an authored PBR material; this has not been profiled on mobile.

[Run 43197538010](https://github.com/kamandbeauty/Ideal/actions/runs/43197538010), commit `4a782faa75153be9a8f523c2248f18f60a822186`, passed headless, rendered and Android jobs. Headless includes the new hit, impact-particle and defeat-collision assertions and the existing gameplay tests. Seven real rendered images are available in [the screenshot artifact](https://github.com/kamandbeauty/Ideal/actions/runs/43197538010/artifacts/13934016290), with the defeat image explicitly test-only. Review of a reduced gameplay frame confirms the hero is no longer completely hidden by the central soldier in that frame, but the hero remains small, the weapons are not clearly readable, the squad is stylized and partially overlaps from this angle, and the clouds remain visibly primitive. A single image does not validate animation quality or motion over time.

The [experimental debug APK](https://github.com/kamandbeauty/Ideal/actions/runs/43197538010/artifacts/13934020969) passed APK inspection (ARM64, portrait, package `org.example.zombieevolution`); SHA-256 `53723edb4b32782412a857d68f39d8e441201b8b7f01b171f94a55a54cec4d4b`. Physical Android, touch, mobile shader performance, visual quality at full device resolution, and audio listening remain untested. The hero rig is approximate, zombie models still lack authored locomotion/attack/death animation, and the hit/death motions are simple procedural feedback. These changes are an incremental prototype, not a claim of a finished premium production or of achieving a specific budget-equivalent quality.

## Generated asphalt texture follow-up

The previously failed image-generation request was retried successfully. `assets/environment/road_asphalt.png` is the generated source texture, not a gameplay screenshot. `ci/prepare_road_texture.py` creates a 512×512 edge-blended derivative (`road_asphalt_tile.png`); the outermost opposite pixel edges match. The bridge shader samples the derivative in world space so slabs share a consistent texture. The procedural noise shader was replaced, while the bridge layout, collision, camera, controls and combat were left unchanged. ## Pass-through glass gates and soldier shooting clip

The two gate halves are now suspended, thick translucent panels, blue for ×۲ and red for +۳. They have no physics bodies and cannot be selected or blocked by bullets. As the hero crosses the gate plane, the hero's world position is transformed into gate-local coordinates; the corresponding half grants its existing effect once. The exact midpoint belongs to the red +۳ half. Automatic gunfire continues to target enemies or the open path, never the gate. The Persian hint now tells the player to pass through. The existing hero-inclusive doubling and squad cap remain in place. The soldier uses the imported `Shoot_OneHanded` clip briefly on a real shot, then returns to the existing carry walk or idle clip; it is not a full authored animation system.
