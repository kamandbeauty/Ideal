Reference only. Nothing in this folder is ever shipped or copied into the clone.

We do not download Dream Games' marketing screenshots into the repo (they are their
copyrighted assets, and a repo is a publishing surface). Capture your own from your
own device or from the public listing, at human speed, and drop them here:

    S14-tasks.png           district task list
    S16-chest.png           district chest reward
    S17-world-map.png       kingdom globe
    S19-shop.png            coin packs

Where to look for each: the Play Store listing (5 public screenshots), the help center
article for that feature (every article embeds the in-game screenshot), and your own
account. Then compare with `replica-diff/imgdiff.py` once we have screens built —
it compares layout, not colour, so our own palette and art do not count against us.
